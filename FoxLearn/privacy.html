Privacy Policy

Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Data controller: nullbyte, Ukraine. Contact email: reportQDR34@outlook.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, to whom we transmit it, and what your rights are.

2. What data we collect

2.1. Data received from Telegram

When you open the Mini App and use the bot, we receive from Telegram signed login data: Telegram ID, name, username (if any), and Telegram interface language. We do not receive your phone number, contacts, profile photo, or contents of your chats.

2.2. Learning profile data

  • native and target language, current level (A1–B2), level test results;
  • name for the tutor to address you (by default your name from Telegram, can be changed);
  • survey answers: learning goal, interests (up to 3), time per day (the survey can be skipped);
  • progress: completed lessons, your vocabulary (words from completed lessons and from the level);
  • coin earnings log for tasks and purchased hints.

2.3. Game data

Streak and record, last visit date, coin balance, streak freezes, purchased and active skins, used Trial, referral connections (who invited you, whether the reward was issued), used promo codes.

2.4. Payment data

Payment record: plan, number of Stars, Telegram payment identifier (charge_id), date, refund mark. We do not see or store bank card data: payment is processed in Telegram.

2.5. AI chat data

The text of your messages in the chat and up to 10 last context replies are sent from your device to our server and then to the AI provider to generate a response. Chat history is stored on the app side on your device during the session; we do not keep a permanent archive of chats in our database, however messages may temporarily get into technical logs and monitoring systems when failures occur. We also store a counter of your AI requests per day.

For hints to tasks in another language, the hint text from the lesson may be sent to the AI provider (this is our content, not your data).

2.6. Analytics and technical data

  • funnel events (for example: language selection, level test, lesson start and completion, paywall display, invoice creation, payment, reaching the AI limit), linked to your ID, to improve the product;
  • server technical logs (requests, errors, request ID);
  • aggregated performance metrics (Prometheus);
  • when error monitoring is enabled: failure reports via Sentry (we do not enable sending of personal data by default; as of the date of this Policy Sentry is actually disabled because SENTRY_DSN is not set, and send_default_pii is set to False).

2.7. Data in your browser

The Mini App may store minor settings in the Telegram browser's local storage (for example, the last flame level for the holiday animation). Word pronunciation uses the built-in speech synthesis on your device; audio is not sent to us.

3. Why we use data

  • providing the Service: login, lessons, answer checking, vocabulary, AI tutor, personalization (level, goal, interests, name);
  • supporting game features: streak, coins, shop, rewards, referrals;
  • processing payments, activating and accounting for subscriptions, refunds, fraud protection;
  • bot notifications: payment confirmations, subscription changes, admin replies; streak reminders are sent only if this feature is enabled in the Service (one message per day, only when there is a risk of losing the streak);
  • security, abuse prevention, rate limiting;
  • product improvement, bug fixing, analysis of the usage funnel;
  • compliance with legal requirements.

4. Legal bases (for users in the EU/EEA)

  • performance of a contract (providing the Service, paid features) — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR;
  • legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention, basic analytics, product improvement) — Art. 6(1)(f);
  • performance of legal obligations (payment accounting) — Art. 6(1)(c);
  • consent — where it is required (for example, the optional survey): you may withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Transfer is possible to the following recipients:

  • Telegram — the login, messaging and payments platform (Telegram Stars). Telegram's own privacy policy applies.
  • AI provider — NVIDIA (integrate.api.nvidia.com) — processes the texts of your chat messages to generate responses. Default model: meta/llama-3.1-70b-instruct; the exact model for a specific deployment is set in the Service configuration, but processing always takes place via the NVIDIA API. Do not send them sensitive data.
  • Hosting and servers — Cloudflare, Inc. provides hosting and infrastructure for the Service through Cloudflare Workers and/or Pages and may process IP addresses and technical request data to deliver and secure the Service. The database is stored on a server under nullbyte's control (Docker, SQLite).
  • Sentry (only if enabled) — error monitoring. As of the date of this Policy Sentry is disabled (SENTRY_DSN is not set), and sending of personal data (send_default_pii) is disabled.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads the Nunito font from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), so your IP address may be transmitted to Google when the app is opened. Where technically possible, fonts will be moved to our own server to avoid this transfer.
  • Authorities — only when required by law.

Some of these recipients may process data outside the EU/EEA (in particular in the United States). For such transfers we rely on standard contractual clauses and other legally required safeguards applied by the respective providers (including NVIDIA, Google, Telegram and Cloudflare).

6. How long we store data

  • account data, progress, vocabulary, game data — as long as you use the Service, or until deletion at your request;
  • payment records — 5 years or longer if required by accounting and tax legislation;
  • analytics events and technical logs — up to 12 months;
  • database backups — rotation, the last 14 copies are stored, so deleted data may remain in backups until they are replaced.

7. Security

We use verification of signed Telegram data, rate limiting, limited database access, backups and HTTPS. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee full protection. In the event of an incident affecting your rights, we will notify you and the regulator if required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on the legislation of your country, you have the right to: access to your data; rectification; erasure ("right to be forgotten"); restriction of processing; data portability; objection to processing based on legitimate interest; withdrawal of consent; lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority (for users in the EU — with the data protection authority of your country; for Ukraine — the Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights).

To exercise your rights or delete your account and data, write to reportQDR34@outlook.com from your Telegram account. We will respond within 30 days. Deletion of progress, coins and purchased digital goods is irreversible; payment records may be stored if required by law.

If you change the language pair in the app, lesson progress and vocabulary for the previous pair are deleted automatically.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for persons under 13 years of age, and we knowingly do not collect their data. If you believe that a child has provided us with data without parental consent, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Bot notifications

The bot sends service messages about payments, the subscription, Stars refunds, and replies to your commands. Streak reminders (if the feature is enabled) arrive no more than once a day. You may stop the bot or disable notifications in Telegram.

11. Automated decisions and AI

The tutor's substantive answers are generated by an AI model. The level after the test is determined automatically by rules (based on the answers). These processes do not create legal consequences for you; you may change the level manually or retake the test.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The date of the last update is indicated at the beginning. We will notify you of material changes in the Service or via the bot.

13. Contacts

Privacy questions: reportQDR34@outlook.com. Payment questions — the /paysupport command in the bot.
