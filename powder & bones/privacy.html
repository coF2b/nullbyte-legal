Privacy Policy

Updated: October 30, 2026

This page is maintained by nullbyte (hereinafter — "we"). It describes what data we collect when you sign in to our games and applications, and what we do with it.

What Data We Collect

  • Discord profile data that Discord provides during sign-in: your Discord ID, username, avatar, and email address.
  • Your game data: progress, settings, and other information that the game stores in your account.

We do not receive or see your Discord password: sign-in happens on Discord's page.

Why We Need This Data

  • So you can sign in and use a single account across all our games and applications.
  • To store your progress and settings.
  • To contact you regarding your account, if necessary.

Where Data Is Stored

Account data and game data are stored in the Supabase service, which acts as our database and authentication system. Discord sign-in is handled by Discord itself under its own privacy policy.

Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your data or share it with advertising companies. Data is processed only by the services without which the account would not work (Supabase and Discord).

How Long We Keep Data

For as long as your account exists. After account deletion, we remove your data from our database.

Your Rights

You can ask us to show what data we store about you, correct it, or delete your account along with all data. Write to us at the address in the "Contacts" section.

Age

Our services are intended for users who are eligible to use Discord (13 years or older, or higher if required by your country's law).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
